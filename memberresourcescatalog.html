About this catalog

Member Research Resources

Techniques, materials, models, and analysis pipelines that individual RARE.Qc member labs have reported as available to share with the network — collected through the RARE.Qc resource questionnaire, and grouped here by the team that offers them.

Research Field

The broad scientific area the resource belongs to (e.g. Basic Science, Bioinformatics) — its outlined tag and the matching outline on each resource bubble below use the same color per field.

Resource Category

The type of resource — a technique, a biological material, an instrument, or a pipeline. Resources from the same team are grouped into one bubble per category, and each category has its own header color to help you scan the page.

Access Conditions

How the resource can be obtained: Open Access (freely available), or subject to a Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) and/or Data Transfer Agreement (DTA), or another arrangement to discuss with the team.
